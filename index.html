About me

Structured operations.
Practical digital thinking.

My career began in 2010, and since then I have worked across different industries and professions, from merchandising and production coordination to administration, ERP and MIS workflows. That range has sharpened how I plan, document and deliver work.

Since 2020 I have also worked as a freelance graphic designer, digital marketer and website designer for international clients, as a Top Rated, government-certified freelancer on freelance marketplaces. The portfolio below shows the kind of work I deliver: social media content, Facebook and other ad creatives, promotional and explainer videos, websites, branding and print.

My focus is helping brands grow: improving their social media presence with consistent, well-designed content, promotional videos and video editing, and Facebook and other ad campaigns that give businesses a clearer path to more enquiries and sales. Results depend on the product, budget and market, so I start from each brand’s goal.

My background in administration, ERP support and merchandising is why I plan carefully, communicate clearly and deliver on deadlines. It also suits hospitality: smoother back-office routines and more consistent promotion of rooms, restaurants and seasonal offers. I am open to freelance projects and to hospitality roles.
